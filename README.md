# MTP
Machine-Trust-Protocol
Derivation: A synthesis of Scuta (Latin: Shields, plural of Scutum ) and Tutamen (Latin: Defense, Protection, or Means of Safety ).   
Thematic Fit: It literally translates to "The Instrument of Shielding" or "The Defense of Shields." 
It captures the plurality of the "Scuta" (comprehensive coverage) and the active state of "Tutamen" (unconquered defense).
